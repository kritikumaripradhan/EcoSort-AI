02 / WHO IS AFFECTED?

Students can learn the habit early.

Schools and colleges generate everyday waste through food, packaging, paper, plastic bottles and other materials. Students are also in a unique position to develop better waste habits and carry them beyond the classroom.

01 / EVERYDAY WASTE

Waste is part of campus life

From snack wrappers and bottles to leftover food and paper, students interact with different forms of waste throughout their daily routines.

02 / CONFUSION

Not every item is obvious

A simple-looking item can sometimes leave students unsure about where it belongs. Clear and accessible information can make these decisions easier.

03 / LEARNING

Good habits start with awareness

When students understand why waste needs to be separated, segregation becomes more than just putting something into a different bin.

04 / FUTURE IMPACT

Small habits can travel further

Students can carry what they learn at school or college into their homes, communities and future workplaces.
