01 / WHO IS AFFECTED?

The waste problem starts at home.

Households are where most everyday waste begins. From food leftovers to plastic packaging, batteries and old electronics, people constantly make decisions about what to throw away and where.

01 / DAILY LIFE

Too many waste decisions

An average household deals with several different kinds of waste every day. The difficulty is not always producing waste, but knowing how each item should actually be separated.

02 / CONFUSION

"Which bin does this go into?"

Packaging, food containers, used tissues, batteries and mixed-material products can make segregation confusing, especially when clear information is not available.

03 / CONSEQUENCE

Small mistakes add up

When different waste streams are mixed, useful recyclable material can become harder to recover and other waste can become more difficult to handle safely.

04 / THE HUMAN SIDE

The burden doesn't end at the bin

Poor segregation can shift the difficulty downstream to waste collectors, sorting workers and the wider waste-management system.
