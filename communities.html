03 / WHO IS AFFECTED?

Cleaner communities start with shared habits.

Waste management becomes a community challenge when individual waste decisions affect shared spaces. Streets, apartment areas, public spaces and neighbourhood collection points all depend on better segregation practices.

01 / SHARED SPACES

Waste doesn't stay personal

Once waste leaves a household, it becomes part of a larger collection system. Poor segregation can therefore affect shared bins, collection points and surrounding spaces.

02 / MIXED WASTE

One mixed bag affects many

When different waste types are placed together, sorting becomes more difficult and materials that could have been recovered may become harder to separate.

03 / COLLECTIVE ACTION

Everyone plays a part

Waste segregation works better when households, students, local communities and collection systems follow compatible practices.

04 / AWARENESS

Information can improve participation

Simple guidance can help people make more informed decisions instead of guessing where an unfamiliar item should go.
