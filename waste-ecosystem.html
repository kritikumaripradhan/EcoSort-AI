04 / WHO IS AFFECTED?

Waste doesn't end when we throw it away.

Behind every discarded item is a larger waste-management ecosystem involving collection, transportation, sorting, recovery and disposal. Better segregation at the beginning can make these stages easier to manage.

01 / COLLECTION

Waste collectors deal with what we leave behind

When waste is mixed, collection and handling can become more difficult. Clear separation at the source can reduce unnecessary mixing of different waste streams.

02 / SORTING

Mixed waste makes sorting harder

Recyclable, organic and other waste streams can require different handling. Separating them earlier can make downstream sorting more manageable.

03 / RECOVERY

Valuable material can be lost

Materials that could potentially be recovered or recycled are easier to manage when they are kept separate from incompatible waste.

04 / THE BIGGER SYSTEM

Every stage is connected

Households, communities, collectors, sorting facilities and disposal systems are all connected. A better decision at the source can support the wider waste-management process.
