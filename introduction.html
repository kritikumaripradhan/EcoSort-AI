01 / INTRODUCTION

Waste is everywhere. Understanding it shouldn't be.

EcoSort AI is a simple AI-powered concept designed to help people understand what kind of waste they have, where it belongs, and how it should be handled responsibly.

Instead of making waste segregation confusing, EcoSort AI turns it into a simple interaction between a person, an image, and an intelligent system.

So, what exactly is EcoSort AI?

Think of it as a digital guide for everyday waste.

We come across different kinds of waste every single day. A plastic bottle, a food wrapper, an old charger, a used battery or even leftover food.

The problem is that knowing something is "waste" does not always mean knowing where it actually belongs.

EcoSort AI aims to bridge that gap. The user provides an image of an object and the system helps identify its likely waste category and provides guidance for responsible segregation.
