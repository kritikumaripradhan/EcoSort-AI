BEHIND THE IDEA

It started with one question.

Not every waste item comes with instructions. Sometimes the hardest part isn't throwing something away. It's knowing what to do with it in the first place.

01 / THE MOMENT

The bin is right there. So why is the decision so hard?

Imagine finishing a drink and holding the empty bottle. Easy, right?

Now imagine an old charger, a food-stained pizza box, a used tissue, a battery, or a packet with multiple materials.

Suddenly, that simple decision becomes: "Wait... where does this actually go?"

THE EVERYDAY STRUGGLE

"I know I should segregate it... but I don't know how."

This is where the idea behind EcoSort AI begins. Waste segregation isn't always a matter of willingness. Sometimes people simply don't have enough information at the moment they need it.

🤔 "Which bin? What category? What now?"
HOW THE IDEA EVOLVED

From confusion to a simple solution.

01 / NOTICE

We noticed the confusion.

Everyday waste can look simple, but deciding how and where to dispose of it isn't always obvious.

02 / ASK

What if AI could help?

Instead of searching through complicated information every time, why not let someone simply show the item and ask?

03 / BUILD

EcoSort AI.

An approachable AI-powered idea that connects image recognition with understandable waste segregation guidance.

WHY AI?

Because asking should be easy.

EcoSort AI is designed around a simple interaction: show the waste item, understand its likely category, and get guidance on what to do next.

👀 See Upload an image of the item you're unsure about.
🧠 Understand AI helps interpret the item and its likely waste category.
♻️ Act Use the result to make a more informed disposal decision.
THE IDEA IN ONE LINE

Make the right choice easier.

EcoSort AI isn't about making waste complicated. It's about making the information needed to deal with it responsibly more accessible.
